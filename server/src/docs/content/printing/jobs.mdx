Every document sent to a printer becomes a print job. Use the **Print jobs** page to follow your jobs, cancel or retry them, and download what was printed.

## Find your jobs

Open [**Print jobs**](/jobs) from the sidebar. Jobs are listed newest first, and the list updates by itself.

To narrow the list, pick a **Printer** or a **Status** at the top. Select **Show more** at the bottom to load older jobs.

**Home** also shows the five most recent jobs under **Recent print jobs**. Select **See all print jobs** to open the full list.

Each job shows:

- the document's title, size, and page count;
- the printer;
- its status, with a message when there is one;
- when it was sent, and where it came from.

The origin reads like **From website**, **From email**, or **From Front desk Mac (alex)**. Jobs from the API, AI assistants, the local network, and test pages say so the same way.

Everyone in a workspace sees every job. With [Printer access](/docs/extensions/printer-access) turned on, you see your own jobs plus jobs on printers where you have **See everyone’s jobs**.

## Job status

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Uploading** | The document is still on its way to the server. |
| **Waiting** | The server has the job. It waits for the device, the printer, or its turn. |
| **Sending to printer** | The device next to the printer has the job. |
| **Printing** | The printer is printing it. |
| **Printed** | Done. The page count shows when the printer reports it. |
| **Didn't print** | Something stopped it. The message under the status says what. |
| **Canceled** | Someone canceled it. |

A job waits while its device is offline or paused, and prints when the device is back. A job that has been tried more than once shows **Try 2**, **Try 3**, and so on.

## Cancel a job

Select **Cancel** next to a job that hasn't finished.

- A job still on the server is canceled right away.
- A job the device already has shows **Canceling…** until the device confirms.
- If the device went offline, the job is canceled with **Canceled while the device was offline**.

## Print a job again

Select **Try again** next to a job that didn't print or was canceled. The job goes back to **Waiting** and prints again with the same document and settings.

**Try again** appears only while the server still has the document.

## Download the document

Select the download button next to a job to get the document that was sent. It is available until the server deletes the document.

## Remove a job from the list

Select the trash button next to a finished job, then select **Remove**. The job disappears from the list, and its document is deleted.

## Who can do what

| Action | Members | Owners and admins |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See jobs | Every job | Every job |
| Cancel | Their own jobs | Any job |
| Try again | Their own jobs | Any job |
| Download | Every job | Every job |
| Remove from the list | No | Any finished job |

With [Printer access](/docs/extensions/printer-access) turned on, permissions on each printer decide instead. You can always cancel your own jobs. Trying again also needs **Print** on the printer. Handling other people's jobs, and removing any job, needs **Manage everyone’s jobs**.

## How long jobs and documents last

Jobs stay in the list until someone removes them. The documents don't.

| Setting | Default | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Keep documents for** | 24 hours | After a job finishes, its document is deleted. You can no longer download it or try again. |
| **Keep trying for** | 24 hours | A job that makes no progress for this long stops with **Didn't print**. |

Owners and admins change both in [Workspace settings](/docs/workspaces/settings#print-jobs).

An upload that doesn't finish within an hour stops with **The upload never finished**.

## When a job doesn't print

Read the message under **Didn't print**. It comes from the device or the server, for example:

- **Gave up after 24 hours without finishing**: the printer or its device stayed unavailable.
- **The upload never finished**: the file never reached the server.

Fix the cause, then select **Try again**.

A job canceled with **Printer removed** was waiting when someone forgot its printer. It can't print again. [Troubleshooting](/docs/reference/troubleshooting) covers more messages.
