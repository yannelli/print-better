A workspace is a group of people who share printers and devices. Invite people to it, and choose whether each one can change settings or only print.

## Roles

Each member has one role in the workspace.

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Member** | Print, and manage their own print jobs. They see every printer and every job. |
| **Admin** | Everything a member can do. They also manage printers, devices, members, and workspace settings. |
| **Owner** | Full control. Only owners can make someone an owner, turn [extensions](/docs/extensions) on or off, and delete the workspace. |

The person who creates a workspace is its first owner. A workspace always has at least 1 owner.

People can belong to more than one workspace, with a different role in each.

## Invite Someone

Owners and admins can invite people.

<Steps>

### Open Members

Open [**Members**](/members) from the sidebar.

### Start an Invite

Select **Invite someone**.

### Choose a Role

Under **What can they do?**, pick **Member** or **Admin**. Owners can also pick **Owner**.

### Add Their Email

**Their email (optional)** is for your records. If the server can send email, Print Better also emails them the link.

### Create the Link

Select **Create invite link**. Copy the **Invite link** and send it to them by text or email.

</Steps>

The link works once and expires after 7 days. The person opens it, creates an account or signs in, and joins right away.

Invites that haven't been used appear under **Waiting to join**. Select **Cancel invite** to stop one from working.

## Change Someone's Role

On the **Members** page, pick a new role in the menu next to the person. The change applies right away.

- Admins can switch people between **Member** and **Admin**.
- Only owners can make someone an owner, or change an owner's role.
- The last owner can't stop being an owner. Make someone else an owner first.

## Remove Someone

Select **Remove** next to the person, then confirm with **Remove**. Admins can't remove owners.

They lose access to the workspace and its printers. Their past print jobs stay in the list.

## Leave a Workspace

Select **Leave** next to your own name, then confirm with **Leave workspace**. The last owner can't leave.

You lose access to its printers. To come back, someone has to invite you again.

## Groups

With [Printer Access](/docs/extensions/printer-access) turned on, the **Members** page has a **Groups** tab. Groups let you give several people and devices the same printer access at once. See [Create a Group](/docs/extensions/printer-access#create-a-group).
