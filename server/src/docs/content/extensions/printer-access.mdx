Printer access lets you choose who can see each printer, who can print to it, and who can manage it. Set it for people, groups, and devices, one printer at a time or for all of them.

Without printer access, every member sees every printer, prints to every added printer, and sees every job. Owners and admins can change everything.

## Turn On Printer Access

Printer access is an [extension](/docs/extensions). A server admin makes it available under **Server admin** > **Extensions**, then a workspace owner turns it on in **Settings** > [**Extensions**](/settings/extensions). See [Turn On an Extension](/docs/extensions#turn-on-an-extension).

<Callout type="note" title="Turning It on Changes Nothing by Itself">

Every printer stays open to everyone with the **User** role, which matches the usual rules. Access changes only when you restrict a printer or change what everyone gets.

</Callout>

## Who Can Get Access

| Who | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone** | Every member and every device in the workspace. Set it per printer, or for all printers at once. |
| **Person** | One member. Their API tokens and AI assistants act as them. |
| **Group** | A named set of members and devices. |
| **Device** | One paired computer or Raspberry Pi. It decides which printers that computer's print window shows. |

Workspace owners and admins always have every permission on every printer. This can't be changed, so nobody can lock the workspace out.

## How Access Adds Up

Someone's permissions on a printer are everything they get from:

- What **Everyone** gets on that printer
- Access given on that printer to them, to a group they're in, or to their device
- Access given the same ways on every printer

Access only adds up, and no rule takes access away. To keep someone out of a printer, restrict it and give access only to the people who need it.

Someone with no permissions on a printer doesn't see it at all. It's left out of their lists, and the API responds as if it doesn't exist.

## Restrict a Printer

A restricted printer is one that **Everyone** can't use. Only owners, admins, and people you choose can.

<Steps>

### Open the Printer's Access Tab

Open the printer from [**Printers**](/printers) and select the **Access** tab. You need **Choose who has access** on the printer to see it.

### Set What Everyone Gets

Under **Everyone**, set **Everyone gets** to **No access**. The page says **This printer is restricted.** Printer lists mark it **Restricted**.

### Give Access to the People Who Need It

Under **People, groups, and devices**, find **Give access**. Search for a person, group, or device, pick a role, and select **Add**.

</Steps>

To change someone's role, pick a new one next to their name. To take it away, select **Remove**. They keep whatever **Everyone** and their groups still get.

**Everyone gets** offers **Workspace default** followed by the default role, each role, and **No access**.

### Restrict Every Printer

Open **Settings** > [**Printer access**](/settings/printer-access) and set **Everyone gets** to **No access**. The change applies right away.

Every printer that doesn't choose its own **Everyone** setting is now restricted. Give access on each printer, or on [every printer](#give-access-to-every-printer).

## Create a Group

Groups give several people and devices the same access at once. Someone can be in more than one group.

<Steps>

### Open Groups

Open [**Members**](/members) and select the **Groups** tab.

### Create the Group

Select **New group**. Enter a **Name**, such as "Front office", and an optional **Description**. Select **Create group**.

### Add People and Devices

In the group's card, search under **Add a person or device**. Pick one and select **Add**.

### Give the Group Access

Open a printer's **Access** tab. Under **Give access**, pick the group and a role, then select **Add**.

</Steps>

Select **Rename** on a group's card to change its name or description. Select **Delete**, then **Delete group**, to remove it. Access given to the group goes with it. Its people and devices keep the access they get in other ways.

## Give Access to Every Printer

Open **Settings** > [**Printer access**](/settings/printer-access) and find **Access to every printer**. Under **Give access**, pick a person, group, or device and a role, then select **Add**.

This covers every printer in the workspace, including printers added later. Each printer's **Access** tab lists it under **From workspace settings**.

To change what **Everyone** gets on every printer, use **Everyone gets** at the top of the same tab instead.

## Give a Device Access

A device's access decides 2 things:

- Which printers appear in that computer's [print window](/docs/printing/print-window)
- Which printers it may send jobs to

Devices are part of **Everyone**. Until you restrict a printer, every device can print to it.

To let one computer use a restricted printer, open the printer's **Access** tab. Under **Give access**, pick the device, or a group it belongs to, with a role that includes **Print**.

To see everything one device can print to, open it from [**Devices**](/devices). Its **Printer access** section lists each printer on your other devices. Only owners and admins see this section.

Each printer shows **Can print**, with the reason, or **Not allowed on this Mac** (**Not allowed on this computer** on Linux). Select **Change access** to open that printer's **Access** tab.

The print window updates on its own. A job the computer sends without access stops in its print queue with **You can't print to this printer from this computer**.

## Make a Custom Role

Owners and admins can make roles for needs the built-in ones don't cover.

<Steps>

### Start a New Role

Open **Settings** > [**Printer access**](/settings/printer-access). Under **Printer roles**, select **New role**.

### Name It

Enter a **Name**, such as "Front desk". The **Description** is optional. People see it when they pick a role.

### Choose Its Permissions

Under **What can people with this role do?**, tick the permissions. Permissions another one includes are ticked and locked.

### Create It

Select **Create role**.

</Steps>

To change a role, select **Edit**, then **Save role**. Everyone who has the role changes at once.

To delete a role, select **Delete**, then **Delete role**. You can delete a role only when nothing uses it: No one may have it, and no printer or workspace default may give it to **Everyone**.

Built-in roles can't be changed or deleted. Each role needs its own name, which can't be a built-in role's name. Each group needs its own name too.

## Check Someone's Access

Open a printer's **Access** tab and find **Check someone’s access**. Under **Who**, search for a person, group, or device.

The result says what they can do, for example "Alex can see it and print". A checklist shows each of the 6 permissions. **Why** lists every reason, such as:

- **Everyone has the User role on this printer**
- **In the Front office group, which has the Manager role on every printer**
- **Workspace admin, which allows everything on every printer**

## Who Can Change Access

| Change | Who |
| --- | --- |
| A printer's **Everyone** setting and the people, groups, and devices on it | Owners, admins, and anyone with **Choose who has access** on that printer |
| Groups, roles, access to every printer, and what **Everyone** gets by default | Owners and admins |
| Turning printer access on or off for the workspace | Owners |

## What Changes When It Is On

| Where | With printer access on |
| --- | --- |
| Printer lists and printer pages | Show only printers you can see. |
| Printing from the web app, the API, AI assistants, and test pages | Needs **Print**. So does **Beep or flash**. |
| [Email to print](/docs/printing/email) | Members need **Print**. Other senders can print only where **Everyone** has **Print**. |
| A computer's print window | Shows a printer only when that device has **Print**. |
| **Print jobs** | Your own jobs, plus jobs on printers where you have **See everyone’s jobs**. |
| Cancel and try again | Your own jobs, or any job with **Manage everyone’s jobs**. |
| Remove from the list | Needs **Manage everyone’s jobs**, also for your own jobs. |
| Printer settings, adding, stopping, forgetting, and sharing | Needs **Change settings**. |

The API and AI assistants follow the same rules. A printer someone can't see returns `404`. A missing permission returns `403` with the code `printer_access_denied`. See [REST API](/docs/reference/rest-api) and [MCP Tools](/docs/reference/mcp).

<Callout type="warning" title="Local Network Sharing">

A printer [shared on the local network](/docs/printers/lan-sharing) accepts jobs from anyone on that network, without signing in. Printer access can't stop that. Turn off **Share on the local network** for printers you restrict.

</Callout>

## Turn Printer Access Off

A workspace owner turns it off in **Settings** > [**Extensions**](/settings/extensions), then selects **Turn off**. A server admin can also turn it off for the whole server.

Everyone gets the usual access to every printer right away. Your groups, roles, and access choices are kept. Turn it back on and they apply again.
