Print rules and limits checks every print job before it's queued. Rules can change a job, send it to another printer, or refuse it. Limits cap how much each person, group, device, or printer prints in a day, week, month, quarter, or year.

## Turn on Print Rules and Limits

Print rules and limits is an [extension](/docs/extensions). A server admin makes it available under **Server admin** > **Extensions**, then a workspace owner turns it on in **Settings** > [**Extensions**](/settings/extensions). See [Turn on an Extension](/docs/extensions#turn-on-an-extension).

<Callout type="note" title="Turning It on Changes Nothing by Itself">

There are no rules or limits until you add them, so jobs print as before.

</Callout>

Owners and admins manage rules and limits under **Usage** in the navigation. The currency, time zone, and prices are in **Settings** > [**Prices**](/settings/prices).

## Where It Applies

Every job is checked, wherever it comes from: the web app, the API, AI assistants, email to print, a computer's print window, and test pages. Jobs already printing are never stopped, and LAN sharing isn't checked.

Rules and limits apply to owners and admins too, unless one is set to **Doesn't apply to owners and admins**.

## Add a Rule

<Steps>

### Open Rules

Open **Usage**, select the **Rules** tab, then select **New rule**.

### Choose When It Applies

Under **When**, select **Add a condition**. A job has to match every condition. A rule without conditions applies to every job.

### Choose What It Does

Under **Then**, pick the actions. They run in order.

### Save It

Select **Add rule**. New rules go at the bottom.

</Steps>

Rules run top to bottom. Each rule whose conditions all match applies its actions, and later rules see the job as earlier ones left it. A rule that blocks stops the job right there. Use the arrows to change the order, and the switch to turn a rule off.

| Condition | Matches when |
| --- | --- |
| **Who sent it** | It's from one of these people, groups, or devices. Groups need [Printer Access](/docs/extensions/printer-access). |
| **Where it came from** | It came from the web app, the API, AI assistants, email, a computer's print window, or test pages. |
| **Printer** | It goes to one of these printers. |
| **Pages** | Its pages, counting copies, are at least or at most a number. |
| **Copies** | It asks for at least this many copies. |
| **Color** | It prints in color, or in black and white. A job set to automatic on a color printer prints in color. |
| **Sides** | It asks for one-sided or two-sided printing. |
| **File type** | It's a PDF, an image, or text. |
| **File size** | Its file is at least this many MB. |
| **Time** | It's sent on these days, between these times, in the workspace time zone. An end before the start runs past midnight. |

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Block** | Refuses the job with your message, shown wherever it was sent from. |
| **Print two-sided** | Long edge, when the printer can. |
| **Print in black and white** | Prints in black and white. |
| **Limit copies** | At most this many copies. |
| **Send to another printer** | Moves the job, only if the sender may print there. Otherwise it stays, with a note. |
| **Add a note** | Shows your note on the job. |

Every change a rule makes shows under the job in [Print Jobs](/docs/printing/jobs), for example "Big jobs on both sides: Printed two-sided".

## Add a Limit

<Steps>

### Open Limits

Open **Usage**, select the **Limits** tab, then select **New limit**.

### Choose Who It Counts

| Choice | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Each member** | Every member has their own count. |
| **One member** | That member's jobs. |
| **A group, shared** | 1 count for everyone in the group. Needs Printer Access. |
| **Each member of a group** | Everyone in the group has their own count. Needs Printer Access. |
| **Each device** | Jobs from each computer's print window. |
| **One device** | That device's jobs. |
| **One printer** | Every job sent to it, from anyone. |
| **The whole workspace** | Every job. |

### Choose the Amount

Pick what it counts (pages, sheets of paper, jobs, or cost), how much, and when it starts over: every day, week, month, quarter, or year. Counts start over at midnight in the workspace time zone.

### Choose What Happens

Under **When a job would go over**, pick **Refuse the job** or **Print it with a warning**, then select **Add limit**.

</Steps>

A job is checked against every limit that counts it, together with everything already printed or waiting this cycle. A refused job says what's left and when the count starts over, for example "Over the limit Monthly pages: this job needs 30 pages and 20 pages are left this month. It starts over on Dec 1."

Pages are printed sides, times copies. A job counts as soon as it's sent, and the count is corrected with the pages the printer reports when it ends. Failed and canceled jobs count only what printed.

## Give Extra for This Cycle

Select **Extra allowance** on a limit. For limits that keep a count for each member or device, pick whose count. The extra ends when the count starts over, and it shows in [Activity](/docs/workspaces/activity).

## What Everyone Sees

- **Usage** > **Overview**: the limits that count your jobs, with what's left. Owners and admins also see every limit's counts, for this cycle or the last.
- **Home**: the limit closest to running out, while a limit counts your jobs.
- The print dialog: what the job leaves you, or why it would be refused. **What happens to this job** has the details.

## Turn It Off

Rules and limits stop applying right away. Rules, limits, extra allowances, prices, and usage history are kept, so turning it back on restores them.
