Passwords and passkeys always work on your server. Google and other single sign-on providers are optional.

## Who can create an account

New accounts are invite-only by default. A workspace owner or admin sends an invite link from [Members](/docs/workspaces/members), and the person creates an account from that link.

To let anyone with your server's address sign up:

1. Open **Server admin**, then **General**.
2. Under **Who can create an account**, choose **Anyone with the address**.
3. Select **Save changes**.

People who sign up without an invite name their own new workspace after they sign up.

Single sign-on follows the same rule. Without an invite, a new Google or OpenID Connect sign-in works only when sign-up is open.

## Passwords

A password needs at least 8 characters. A username has 2 to 32 letters, numbers, dots, dashes, or underscores. People sign in with their username or their email address.

The server hashes passwords with scrypt and a server-wide secret, `AUTH_PEPPER`. After 10 failed attempts for one account within 15 minutes, the server refuses further attempts for that account until the 15 minutes pass.

## Passkeys

A passkey signs you in with a fingerprint, face, or device PIN. Anyone can create an account with a passkey and no password.

To add a passkey:

1. Open **Your account**, then **Sign-in and security**.
2. Under **Passkeys**, select **Add a passkey**.

To sign in with it, select **Sign in with a passkey** on the sign-in page.

<Callout type="warning">

A passkey works only on the hostname where it was created. Choose your final domain before people register passkeys. See [Use your own domain](/docs/self-hosting/deploy#use-your-own-domain).

</Callout>

You can't remove your last way to sign in. Add a password, another passkey, or a connected account first.

## Sign in with Google

<Steps>

### Create an OAuth client

In Google Cloud Console, open **APIs & Services**, then **Credentials**. Create an OAuth client ID of type **Web application**.

### Add the redirect address

Add this address under **Authorized redirect URIs**:

```text
https://your-print-better.example/api/auth/sso/google/callback
```

**Server admin** shows the same address as **Redirect address**, ready to copy.

### Turn it on

Open **Server admin**, then **Sign-in options**. Under **Sign in with Google**, paste the **Client ID** and **Client secret**. Turn on **Show this option on the sign-in page**, then select **Save changes**.

</Steps>

## Sign in with your organization

This works with Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, Authentik, Keycloak, and other OpenID Connect providers.

<Steps>

### Register an app with your provider

Create a confidential web client. Use this redirect address:

```text
https://your-print-better.example/api/auth/sso/oidc/callback
```

Print Better asks for the scopes `openid email profile`.

### Enter the details

Open **Server admin**, then **Sign-in options**. Under **Sign in with your organization**, fill in:

- **Button name**: People see "Continue with" followed by this name.
- **Issuer address**: The `https` address of your provider. Its discovery document must be at the issuer address plus `/.well-known/openid-configuration`.
- **Client ID** and **Client secret**.

### Turn it on

Turn on **Show this option on the sign-in page**, then select **Save changes**. The server checks the issuer's discovery document when you save.

</Steps>

## How single sign-on finds an account

When someone signs in with Google or your organization, the server looks for an account in this order:

1. An account already connected to that Google or organization account.
2. An account whose verified email matches the provider's verified email. The two are connected right away.
3. A new account, when the person has an invite or sign-up is open.

If an account has that email but hasn't verified it, sign-in stops. The person signs in another way first and connects the provider from their account.

## Connect an existing account

Once Google or your organization sign-in is on, people can connect it to the account they have:

1. Open **Your account**, then **Sign-in and security**.
2. Under **Connected accounts**, choose the provider.

A connected account is also a backup way in.

## Getting back in

| Way back in | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Recovery codes | Under **Sign-in and security**, select **Create recovery codes**. You get 10 codes. Each works once, and new codes replace the old ones. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot your password?** and enter a code. |
| Reset link by email | On **Forgot your password?**, use **Email me a reset link**. The link works for 1 hour. This needs [outbound email](/docs/self-hosting/email). |
| Reset link from an admin | In **Server admin**, then **People**, select **Reset password** next to the person. Send them the one-time link. It works for 24 hours. |

Setting a new password from a reset link signs out every other session.
