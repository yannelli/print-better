This page explains how a device finds printers and what happens to each new printer it finds. Use it to understand why a printer appears, and to turn discovery methods on or off.

## Where a device looks

A device uses four methods. All four are on by default.

| Method | Setting on the device's page | What it finds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bonjour | **Network printers (Bonjour)** | Printers that announce IPPS, IPP, raw port 9100, or LPD on the local network. Most printers made in the last 15 years do. |
| SNMP | **Older network printers (SNMP)** | Office printers that don't announce themselves. The device asks the network who is a printer. |
| USB | **USB printers** | Printers plugged into the device with a cable. |
| System printers | **Printers already set up on this computer** | Printers in the computer's own print system, CUPS on a Mac or Linux computer. |

When two methods find the same physical printer, the device merges them into one printer with several routes. When it prints, it tries the routes in order. If a route can't connect, it moves to the next one.

Printers that Print Better added to the print window from your other devices are never reported back as new printers.

## When a device looks

- When Print Better starts.
- Every 10 minutes.
- When you ask: **Look now** on the device's page, **Find printers** in the menu bar icon or on the status page, or `printbetter discover` on the device.

A device also checks each added printer every 60 seconds. It reports the printer's state, problems such as low toner, and supply levels. See [Status and supplies](/docs/printers/status).

If a printer stops showing up, it stays in the list with its settings and shows **Not responding**. Remove it yourself if it is gone for good.

## New printers

Each printer a device finds for the first time is **found**. It can't take jobs until someone adds it. The **Add printers automatically** setting in workspace settings decides what happens next:

| Choice | Adds |
| --- | --- |
| **Recommended** (the default) | Printers plugged into the device, plus network printers when the device finds 3 or fewer of them. |
| **All of them** | Every printer the device finds. |
| **Ask me first** | Nothing. Found printers wait under **Found but not added** for you to add them. |

Each device can follow the workspace or choose for itself. On the device's page, under **New printers**, **Add new printers automatically** offers **Use the workspace setting** (the default), **Add the likely ones**, **Add every printer found**, and **Ask me first**.

To add a found printer yourself, see [Add a printer](/docs/printers/add).

## A printer two devices can see

When a second device finds a printer that another device already added, the printer doesn't show up again under **Found but not added**. The server matches them by the printer's own ID.

## Turn a method off

Turn off a method you don't need, for example SNMP on a network with many printers that aren't yours.

<Tabs>

<Tab label="Web app">

Open the device's page. Under **Where to look for printers**, turn off the method.

</Tab>

<Tab label="On the device">

Use the status page at `http://127.0.0.1:47631`, or the command line:

```sh
printbetter config set discovery.snmp off
```

The keys are `discovery.mdns`, `discovery.snmp`, `discovery.usb`, and `discovery.os`.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

With every method off, the checkup reports **Discovery** as off instead of a problem.

## Look without a server

`printbetter discover` works on a device that isn't connected yet. It prints each printer's name, model, key, and routes. Use `--timeout` to look longer, up to 60 seconds:

```sh
printbetter discover --timeout 10
```
