Check whether a printer is ready, see how much ink or toner it has left, and make it show you where it is.

## Printer status

Every printer card and printer page shows a status. Devices check each added printer once a minute and report changes right away.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready** | The printer is on and waiting for work. |
| **Printing** | The printer is printing. |
| **Needs attention** | The printer stopped. Its own message, if it sends one, appears under the status. |
| **Not responding** | The device can't reach the printer. Check that the printer is on and connected. |
| **Offline** | The device that connects the printer is turned off or not connected. |
| **Checking** | The device hasn't reported the printer's state yet. |

When the printer reports a problem that stops printing, the status names the problem instead. For example **Out of paper**, **Paper jam**, **Out of toner**, or **The cover is open**.

Warnings that don't stop printing appear under the status. For example **Toner is low** or **Paper is running low**.

When an added printer needs attention, a dot appears next to **Printers** in the sidebar.

### Common problems

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **Out of paper** or **Load paper** | Add paper to the tray the printer asks for. |
| **Paper jam** | Open the printer and clear the jam. |
| **Out of toner**, **Out of ink or toner** | Replace the cartridge. |
| **A door is open** or **The cover is open** | Close every door and cover. |
| **Paper tray is missing** | Push the tray all the way in. |
| **Output tray is full** | Take the printed pages out. |
| **Waste box is full** | Replace the waste toner or ink box. |
| **Drum needs replacing** | Replace the drum unit. |
| **Paused** | Resume the printer, on the printer itself or in the device's own printer settings. |
| **Missing a printer driver** | The computer's print system can't drive this printer. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/reference/troubleshooting). |

Problems Print Better doesn't recognize appear in the printer's own words.

### The Status section

Open a printer to see its **Status** section. It says **Everything looks good. This printer is ready.** when nothing is wrong. Otherwise it lists each problem, worst first.

If the device is offline, the section says so. Jobs wait until the device is back.

When the printer sends a message of its own, it appears after **The printer says:**.

## Ink, toner, and other supplies

The **Status** section lists every supply the printer reports, with a level bar and a percentage.

- A supply at 15% or less shows **Low**, for example **Low, 12%**.
- **Level unknown** means the printer reports the supply but not how full it is.
- **This printer doesn't report its ink or toner levels.** means it reports no supplies at all.

Printer cards show up to four supplies.

On a Mac, the print window shows the same levels for printers on your other devices. See [Supply levels on a Mac](/docs/printing/print-window#supply-levels-on-a-mac).

## Check the status now

Open the printer. Under **Tools**, find **Check status now** and select **Check again**.

The device asks the printer for its latest status and ink levels. The device must be online. When it isn't, the button is turned off and the row asks you to turn the device on.

## Make a printer identify itself

Use this to find the right printer in a room full of them.

Open the printer. Under **Tools**, find **Find this printer** and select **Beep or flash**.

The printer beeps, flashes its lights, or shows a message, if it can. The device must be online. With [Printer access](/docs/extensions/printer-access) turned on, you need the **Print** permission.

## Print a test page

Open the printer. Under **Tools**, find **Test page** and select **Print a test page**.

Print Better sends a one-page PDF with the printer's name, its device, and the time. The job appears in [**Print jobs**](/jobs) as **Test page**.
